Tattoo Style Explorer

Find your style

Tattoo styles vary widely, and individual artists often specialize. Use these categories as a starting point when researching artists and portfolios.

🦅

Traditional

Bold outlines, strong colors and classic imagery such as roses, eagles, daggers and nautical themes.

🌹

Neo-Traditional

Traditional foundations with expanded color palettes, dimension and highly detailed illustrative work.

◐

Black & Grey

Black ink and shading techniques used to create depth, contrast and detailed monochrome artwork.

👁️

Realism

Highly detailed work intended to reproduce portraits, animals, objects or photographic imagery.

〰️

Fine Line

Delicate linework often used for subtle illustrations, botanical designs and detailed minimalist pieces.

🐉

Japanese

Large-scale compositions featuring imagery such as dragons, koi, flowers, waves and mythological themes.

⬛

Blackwork

Bold use of black ink ranging from graphic patterns to large areas of solid black and illustrative designs.

◇

Geometric

Patterns, symmetry, shapes and mathematically inspired designs requiring precise execution.

Aa

Lettering

Words, names, quotations and typography ranging from fine script to bold custom lettering.

🎨

Watercolor

Color-focused designs inspired by watercolor painting, often using splashes, gradients and flowing forms.

⚡

Anime & Pop Culture

Artwork inspired by animation, comics, games, movies and recognizable fictional characters.

•

Minimalist

Clean, restrained designs using simple shapes, symbols and limited linework.

  • Aftercare instructions

Body Art Safety

Safety matters more than the design

Tattooing and piercing involve breaking the skin and can carry health risks. Use authoritative information and follow professional medical advice when appropriate.

⚠️ Tattoo Ink & Safety

Tattooing can involve risks including infection and allergic reactions. Ink contamination and other safety issues can also occur.

InkTat.com does not certify studios or artists as safe, licensed, inspected or approved. Regulations and licensing requirements vary by state and locality.

If you experience symptoms that concern you after tattooing or piercing, contact an appropriate healthcare professional.
